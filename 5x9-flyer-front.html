Strong enough for the road no child should have to take.

Delivering 500 durable backpacks to children displaced by war
Some children are fleeing war — traveling with only what they can carry.

Oceanbags Foundation is delivering 500 ultra-durable backpacks through Partners.ngo, who work directly in refugee camps and border areas.

Each backpack costs $25 to make and deliver. Built to last — waterproof, reinforced, dependable — they give a child something they can trust when almost everything else is uncertain.

Campaign At-A-Glance

Goal: 500 backpacks
Cost per backpack: $25
Partner: Partners.ngo
Material: Ocean Bound Plastics
